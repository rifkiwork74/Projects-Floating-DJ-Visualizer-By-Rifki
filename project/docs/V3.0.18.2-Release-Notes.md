# Floating DJ Visualizer V3.0.18.2

**Audio Source Parity & Capture Stability**  
`versionCode: 97` · Baseline: `3.0.18.1`

## English

V3.0.18.2 improves the consistency of the two supported audio-analysis routes:

- Calibrates PCM playback-capture levels to more closely match Android Visualizer's normalized output-mix behavior.
- Preserves stereo playback information by analyzing left and right channels separately before RMS magnitude combination.
- Accumulates partial AudioRecord reads into exact Hop-sized blocks, keeping the configured FFT window advance stable.
- Uses the configured AudioRecord sink sample rate in PCM frequency, window-duration, and Hop-interval calculations.
- Keeps the same 64-band logarithmic spectrum measurement and time-based response animation across both routes.
- Adds no microphone-input mode and does not bypass source-app capture restrictions.

## Bahasa Indonesia

V3.0.18.2 meningkatkan konsistensi dua jalur analisis audio yang didukung:

- Mengalibrasi level PCM playback capture agar lebih mendekati perilaku normalisasi output-mix Android Visualizer.
- Mempertahankan informasi stereo dengan menganalisis kanal kiri dan kanan secara terpisah sebelum penggabungan magnitudo RMS.
- Mengumpulkan pembacaan AudioRecord parsial hingga satu blok Hop penuh, sehingga pergeseran jendela FFT tetap stabil.
- Menggunakan sample rate sink AudioRecord yang dikonfigurasi dalam perhitungan frekuensi PCM, durasi jendela, dan interval Hop.
- Mempertahankan pengukuran spektrum logaritmik 64-band dan animasi response berbasis waktu pada kedua jalur.
- Tidak menambahkan input mikrofon dan tidak melewati pembatasan capture dari aplikasi sumber.

## Platform notes

- Android 8–9 (API 26–28): system output-mix Visualizer route only.
- Android 10–16 (API 29–36): user-authorized MediaProjection playback capture may be available when the source app allows it and Android's usage, capture-policy, profile, and foreground-service requirements are met.
- Requested Visualizer FFT size may be clamped by the device. The service reports the applied capture size and callback rate.
- Hop Size applies to PCM playback capture. The standard Visualizer callback cadence is determined by the platform.
